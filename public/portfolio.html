    About

    For more than 25 years I've written the documents people actually use at work: step-by-step procedures, training materials, and guides that help teams adopt new tools, including AI.

    That work taught me to explain things plainly, to write for the reader in front of me, and to cut anything that doesn't help. I bring the same habits to blog posts and articles, with a warmer voice.

    Outside the office I run small ventures of my own, from reselling vintage clothing to selling at local farmers markets, so I write about small business from experience.

    How I work

    I write every piece myself. I may use AI tools to help organize an outline or proofread a final draft, and I'm glad to work without them if your project requires it.
